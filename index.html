FE Civil pacing verdict guide

A raw correct-count without time data hides the failure mode. Use this flow after every timed sitting.

1. Split the two sections

The FE Civil paper is two sections with a one-way submit. If section 1 ate 3+ hours, the break will not save section 2. Rehearse on a full-length timed mock.

2. Rank time sinks, not just misses

A 4-minute miss that you eventually got right is still a pacing defect. Flag topics that burned clock even when correct, then drill them via practice problems.

3. Map sinks to knowledge areas

High-ceiling areas (structural, geotech, water, transportation) deserve disproportionate drill. Start from the FE Civil overview and the weight-based study plan.

4. Retake path

If you already have an NCEES diagnostic, skip guessing: use the retake planner and read how the diagnostic is scored.
